# Northstar Digital Knowledge Base

This repository contains a fictional knowledge base created for testing the Amplify OS document explorer, editor, search, revision history, and AI knowledge tools. Everything here is synthetic and intended for demonstration purposes only.

## Fictional company profile

Northstar Digital is a fictional B2B software development and consulting company based in Belgium. The company employs about 35 people and focuses on custom software development, cloud modernization, data integrations, internal business platforms, and AI-assisted workflow automation.

## What is included

The documentation is organized into practical areas such as company direction, onboarding, engineering, projects, sales, marketing, people, security, operations, clients, and reusable templates. The material is written to feel realistic enough for browsing, searching, editing, reviewing, and asking an AI agent questions about how the company operates.

## Metadata and structure

Most documents include YAML front matter with fields such as title, owner, status, review cadence, last review date, tags, and appliesTo. The folder layout is designed to support browsing by department and by topic.

## Intentional test cases

A few inconsistencies are intentionally included so testers can explore contradiction detection, stale content, and knowledge updates. Examples include slightly different meeting cadences, older approval language, and a couple of links that intentionally point to documents that do not yet exist.

## Important note

No real people, customers, credentials, or confidential information are included. This is test data only.
