# Lead Qualification

Northstar Digital uses a pragmatic method for qualifying inbound and outbound leads. The goal is to understand whether the prospect has a clear business need, a realistic timeline, and enough internal support to move forward with a software engagement.

## Qualification questions

Sales reps should confirm at least the following before scheduling a deeper discovery session:

- What business problem is motivating the conversation?
- Which team or teams would be involved in a solution?
- What timeline, budget, and success measure are realistic?
- Is the prospect open to a phased delivery model rather than a large one-off project?

## Qualification rubric

High-fit leads usually have a concrete operational pain point, a decision maker involved in the conversation, and at least one technical or business stakeholder who can support implementation planning. Lower-fit leads are often early in the buying process or are asking for a large transformation without a clear scope.
