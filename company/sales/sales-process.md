# Sales Process

Northstar Digital's sales process is designed to support strong conversations without creating unnecessary bureaucracy. The company wants to move from initial contact to proposal in a way that is clear for both the seller and the prospect.

## Standard flow

1. Initial introduction and qualification.
2. Discovery meeting to confirm business context and project goals.
3. Solution framing and preliminary estimate.
4. Proposal drafting and internal review.
5. Proposal submission and follow-up conversations.

## Internal expectations

Sales reps should keep a short record of major decisions, open questions, and next steps. When a project may involve engineering or implementation planning, the sales lead should involve the project team early rather than waiting until a proposal is already under review. This helps prevent mismatches between promise and delivery.

## Known issue

Some proposals still reference a previous approval model that asks for sign-off from the marketing lead as well as the sales director. That rule is being phased out, but a few older templates still include it. The updated guidance is described in [Proposal Guidelines](proposal-guidelines.md).
