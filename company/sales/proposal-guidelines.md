# Proposal Guidelines

Northstar Digital proposals should be clear, concise, and realistic. They need to communicate the value of the engagement while also setting a credible expectation for the delivery team. The goal is to avoid overpromising and to make the path to implementation visible.

## Required sections

- Business context and objectives
- Suggested approach and timeline
- Deliverables and assumptions
- Project governance and communication plan
- Pricing structure and commercial notes

## Review rhythm

Most proposals should be reviewed by the sales lead and one delivery representative before they are sent. Large or strategic proposals may also require sign-off from the project manager or engineering lead. The review should focus on scope clarity, dependencies, and whether the proposal reflects current delivery capacity.

## Good practice

When a proposal includes AI-assisted workflow components, the team should make sure the assumptions are explicit and that the implementation approach is not treated as a generic add-on. That reduces the chance of later confusion around effort, risk, or support needs.
