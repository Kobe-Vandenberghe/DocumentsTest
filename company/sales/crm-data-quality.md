# CRM Data Quality

Northstar Digital uses the CRM to maintain a practical record of opportunities, accounts, and commercial conversations. The system is not intended to be a perfect archive of every detail, but it should still provide enough context for handoffs, forecasting, and follow-up activities.

## Minimum expectations

- Every opportunity should have a clear owner and a current next step.
- Status labels should reflect the latest known stage rather than old habits.
- Notes should capture significant decisions, risks, and client requests in plain language.
- A follow-up date should be present for active opportunities.

## Common quality issues

The team is still correcting data that was entered before the current sales process was standardized. Some older records contain duplicate notes, outdated status labels, or missing client context. Those records should be updated when they are used for a current conversation or a proposal review.

## Review practice

The sales team should review CRM quality at least once per month, especially around accounts that are close to a decision point. The goal is not to perfect every record immediately, but to keep the data trustworthy enough for internal planning and client follow-up.
