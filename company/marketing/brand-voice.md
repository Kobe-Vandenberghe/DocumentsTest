# Brand Voice

Northstar Digital's brand voice is confident, practical, and quietly optimistic. The company does not write like a startup that is trying to sound futuristic at all costs, nor like a corporate group that hides behind generic language. The tone should feel credible, thoughtful, and clear.

## Voice characteristics

- Straightforward and specific
- Calm rather than dramatic
- Focused on outcomes and evidence
- Respectful of the reader's time

## Things to avoid

- Overstating impact or promising transformations that are not yet in place
- Using buzzwords without explaining them
- Writing in a way that sounds detached from the client reality

## How it applies to content

Marketing and sales content should be written with the audience's context in mind. Internal documents may be more detailed, but even those should remain readable and free from unnecessary filler. The voice can vary slightly across channels, but the company should remain recognizable in all of them.
