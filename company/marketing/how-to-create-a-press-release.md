# How to Create a Press Release

Northstar Digital uses press releases sparingly and only when the story has a clear external angle. The company prefers earned attention over promotional noise, so the process emphasizes accuracy, relevance, and a credible message rather than volume.

## Process overview

1. Confirm the story, audience, and desired outcome.
2. Draft the release using the [Press Release Template](../templates/press-release-template.md).
3. Review the draft with the marketing lead and the relevant business owner.
4. Submit for approval if the content mentions a client, partnership, or major milestone.
5. Publish through the approved channels and track follow-up coverage.

## Approval expectations

For general company announcements, the marketing lead and the CEO review is sufficient. If the release mentions a client project, a product launch, or a significant operational milestone, the sales director should also be included. The approval trail should be recorded in the workspace so the content can be revised later if needed.

## Writing advice

A good press release should answer the who, what, why, and when questions quickly. It should avoid jargon and make the impact of the announcement clear to a non-technical audience. A short quote from a relevant leader can help, but it should not feel scripted or exaggerated.
