# Social Media Guidelines

Northstar Digital uses social media to support visibility and to share practical stories about delivery work, learning, and client outcomes. The channel strategy is intentionally modest. The company would rather publish a handful of relevant updates than flood channels with repetitive content.

## Posting expectations

- Keep posts concise and linked to concrete work or insight.
- Attribute client work carefully and avoid sharing sensitive details.
- Use company-approved visuals when available.
- Add a clear call to action only when it is relevant to the audience.

## Content examples

Examples include a short note about a successful integration launch, a reflection on lessons learned from a project handoff, or a post about safe AI use in workflow automation. The tone should stay consistent with [Brand Voice](brand-voice.md) and should not imply that every client engagement is identical.

## Known gap

This document is still marked as draft because the team has not yet agreed on the final approval path for social posts that reference customer stories. That is one reason the document should be reviewed before it is treated as fully authoritative.
