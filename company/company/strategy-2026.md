# Strategy 2026

Northstar Digital's 2026 strategy focuses on expanding capability in three areas: platform modernization, AI-assisted workflow automation, and integration-led transformation. These priorities align with the company's existing strengths and with the needs of clients that are trying to modernize without replacing every system at once.

The strategy is grounded in a simple operating assumption: growth should come from repeatable delivery, not from overextending the organization. That is why the company is investing in reusable delivery patterns, better project scoping, and a more formal approach to knowledge reuse. The intent is not to become a large bureaucracy; it is to become more dependable as the volume and complexity of client work increases.

## Strategic focus areas

- Build a stronger internal library of delivery templates and reference architectures.
- Grow the number of projects that include AI-assisted workflow components where they add measurable value.
- Improve client communication quality and reduce avoidable escalation points.
- Rebalance staff effort across delivery, operations, and knowledge management.

## Current tensions

The leadership team is aware that some initiatives are still being managed through informal habits. For example, the company has not yet fully decided whether all proposal reviews should route through the same approvals process described in [Proposal Guidelines](../sales/proposal-guidelines.md). That uncertainty is one reason the strategy remains in review.
