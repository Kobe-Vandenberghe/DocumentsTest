# Organizational Structure

Northstar Digital is organized as a lean consulting and product delivery company with a small central leadership layer and a set of cross-functional delivery teams. The company is not divided into large silos; instead, each engagement usually involves a project lead, one or more engineers, a delivery coordinator, and a client-facing representative.

The core leaders are Sophie Lambert, CEO; Thomas Vermeulen, Head of Engineering; Emma De Smet, Sales Director; Lucas Peeters, Marketing Lead; Noah Janssens, Project Manager; Mila Claes, People Operations Lead; and Arthur Dubois, Security Officer. These roles are intentionally broad, and some responsibilities overlap in practice.

## Team model

- Engineering manages delivery standards, technical quality, and operational excellence.
- Projects coordinates delivery cadence, stakeholder communication, and risk escalation.
- Sales and marketing shape pipeline quality, brand positioning, and early-stage client conversations.
- People operations manages onboarding, performance, and internal development.
- Security provides policy guidance and incident oversight but does not own all day-to-day controls.

## Known gaps

A few responsibility boundaries are still not fully formalized. The owner of the internal knowledge hub is currently shared between Operations and Engineering, and the handoff between project delivery and support after launch is still described differently in several documents. This ambiguity is acknowledged and is one of the reasons the company keeps improving its process libraries.
