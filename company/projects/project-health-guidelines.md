# Project Health Guidelines

Project health at Northstar Digital is assessed through a mix of status updates, milestone progress, delivery quality, and stakeholder sentiment. The purpose is not to create a formal scorecard that overpowers the work. It is to make issues visible early enough that the team can respond without surprise.

## Health signals

- Delivery pace compared to plan
- Number of unresolved blockers
- Quality issues or repeated rework
- Stakeholder confidence and escalation activity
- Risk exposure around dependencies or security concerns

## Suggested cadence

The company historically used weekly status meetings for most projects, but some teams have shifted to a two-week rhythm when the delivery pace is steady. This difference is still visible in project updates and should be treated as a known inconsistency until the standard is clarified.

## Escalation triggers

A project should be escalated when a milestone slips without a recovery plan, when a client is repeatedly unhappy with communication, or when quality concerns start affecting delivery confidence. The project lead should then decide whether the issue is best handled through the normal project process or the formal [Escalation Process](../clients/escalation-process.md).
