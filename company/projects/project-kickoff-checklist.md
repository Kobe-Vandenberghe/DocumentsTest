# Project Kickoff Checklist

A strong kickoff creates clarity early and reduces avoidable rework later. Northstar Digital uses a standard checklist to ensure that the delivery team, the client, and stakeholders all agree on the purpose, success criteria, and operating cadence of a project.

## Checklist

- Confirm the client, project name, and project owner.
- Review objectives, scope, assumptions, and constraints.
- Record the target timeline, budget guardrails, and success measures.
- Confirm the communication rhythm and escalation path.
- Identify key dependencies, technical risks, and known unknowns.
- Agree on a working document location, approval path, and next steps.

## Recommended agenda

The kickoff meeting should usually cover goals, scope, roles, reporting, and decision rights. It is also useful to discuss the client’s preferred style of communication and whether any compliance or security requirements will affect implementation choices.

## Practical note

The template in [Project Kickoff Template](../templates/project-kickoff-template.md) should be used whenever possible, but some projects still start with a lighter version when timelines are especially tight. That can be acceptable, as long as the core information is captured before engineering work begins.
