# Project Delivery Process

Northstar Digital manages most client engagements through a practical delivery model that moves from kickoff to handoff without unnecessary ceremony. The company relies on a shared set of expectations for planning, execution, review, and closure so that projects remain consistent even when the underlying technical stack changes.

## Core stages

- Initiation and scoping: confirm goals, assumptions, and initial dependencies.
- Planning: establish milestones, decision points, and communication routines.
- Delivery: execute work in short cycles with regular status updates.
- Review: validate outcomes against acceptance criteria and confirm open issues.
- Handoff: close the project with documentation, responsibility notes, and any follow-up actions.

## Required artifacts

Every project should have a shared project brief, a visible action tracker, and a documented decision log. In addition, client-facing status updates should be concise and should not create confusion about what is complete, what is pending, and what remains uncertain.

## Operating principles

The project lead is responsible for keeping the team aligned, but delivery responsibilities are shared. Engineering, sales, and client communication all influence the final outcome. The company still has some uneven practices when it comes to how much detail is stored in a formal tracker versus in chat threads, but the expectation is that important decisions should be recorded somewhere durable.
