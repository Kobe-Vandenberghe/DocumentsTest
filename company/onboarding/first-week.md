# First Week Guide

The first week at Northstar Digital is designed to help new employees get oriented without overwhelming them. The aim is to create enough familiarity with the organization, working practices, and tools that they can begin contributing safely and confidently.

New hires should complete a short set of introductions, reading tasks, and operational checks during the first five working days. The manager should schedule time with the People Operations lead, the engineering representative, and a peer buddy. The People Operations team also coordinates access requests and local setup steps so the employee can begin using the standard toolchain.

## Suggested week outline

Day 1: Welcome meeting, review of company values, introduction to internal documentation, and basic security expectations.
Day 2: Review of the [Development Environment](development-environment.md), project practices, and communication habits.
Day 3: Learn the project lifecycle and the role of project leads in day-to-day coordination.
Day 4: Review the [Security Training](security-training.md) material and confirm access setups.
Day 5: Complete the onboarding checklist and book a follow-up conversation with the manager.

## Common guidance

We encourage new employees to ask questions early, especially when they are not sure whether a detail belongs in the documentation or in a conversation. The company uses a pragmatic approach: if a process is unclear, raise it and contribute to the next revision.
