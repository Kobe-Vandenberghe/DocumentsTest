# Escalation Process

Northstar Digital uses a defined escalation process when an issue threatens delivery quality, client confidence, or relationship stability. The company wants to avoid letting low-level concerns grow into avoidable disputes.

## When to escalate

Escalate when a project misses a milestone without a recovery plan, a client raises recurring concerns about communication, or a risk threatens confidentiality or service continuity. The escalation should be routed through the project lead and the relevant service owner rather than through ad hoc messages.

## Response path

1. Summarize the issue and document the current impact.
2. Notify the project lead and, when appropriate, the security officer.
3. Align on a response plan and determine whether a decision is needed from the client.
4. Record the outcome in the project workspace.

## Escalation examples

Examples include repeated missed milestones for the Nova Retail inventory platform or a sudden confidentiality concern on the BrightPath Health integration project. In both cases, the response should remain calm, factual, and visible to the appropriate stakeholders.

## Practical note

The company has recently started using a more formal client escalation note for larger engagements, but some older client relationships still rely on lighter updates. That creates a few inconsistencies in practice, especially for projects that involve the Arden Finance compliance dashboard.
