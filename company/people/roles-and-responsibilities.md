# Roles and Responsibilities

Northstar Digital relies on a mixed model of explicit ownership and shared accountability. The company expects people to know the main responsibilities of their role, but it also expects them to coordinate with others when a project or process spans multiple functions.

## Key roles

- CEO: sets strategic direction and validates major company positioning.
- Head of Engineering: owns technical standards and delivery quality across teams.
- Sales Director: leads commercial planning and partnership development.
- Project Manager: keeps delivery moving and coordinates stakeholder communication.
- People Operations Lead: manages onboarding, development, and internal people processes.
- Security Officer: owns policy guidance and incident escalation oversight.
- People Operations Lead: also coordinates remote work expectations and onboarding support.

## Shared ownership areas

Some responsibilities remain intentionally shared. For example, the quality of internal documentation is treated as a collective responsibility, even though Operations and Engineering both have a strong interest in it. Likewise, the final state of a client handoff may be influenced by project delivery, support, and the client-facing lead.

## Known ambiguity

The document still leaves some ownership unclear around support after launch. That is a known issue and should be treated as a reminder to capture decisions explicitly rather than assume consensus.
