# Learning and Development

Northstar Digital encourages continuous learning because the company works in a fast-moving environment and expects employees to combine delivery expertise with good judgment. The company supports learning through budgets for courses, internal workshops, and peer-led knowledge sessions.

## Development categories

- Technical and product skills
- Delivery and project coordination
- Communication and stakeholder management
- Security and compliance awareness
- Leadership and mentoring

## Expectations

Each employee should maintain a simple development plan that covers the next six to twelve months. Managers are expected to discuss these plans during reviews and to support training opportunities when they align to project needs and company priorities.

## Practical examples

A developer might take a course on cloud deployment practices, while a project lead might attend training in facilitation or risk planning. Marketing staff may benefit from a workshop on content systems or analytics, and security engineers may participate in incident response drills.
