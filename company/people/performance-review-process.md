# Performance Review Process

Northstar Digital uses performance reviews as a structured way to reflect on outcomes, development areas, and future goals. Reviews are not intended to be purely evaluative; they are also used to help employees build better habits and plan their next steps.

## Review rhythm

Managers should hold a review conversation at least once per year. For newer employees, a lighter check-in after six months is also recommended. The review should cover delivery quality, collaboration, ownership, and professional growth.

## Preparation expectations

Employees are expected to prepare a short self-review in advance. Managers should gather evidence from project work, peer feedback, and documented contributions before writing the review summary. The company prefers concrete examples over generic praise.

## Development focus

Performance discussions should include at least one learning objective and one practical improvement area. These goals should be connected to the employee's role and to the company's current priorities, such as better handoffs, clearer communication, or stronger knowledge sharing.
