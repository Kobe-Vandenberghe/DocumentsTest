# Remote Work Policy

Northstar Digital supports a hybrid working model for most employees, with a strong preference for in-person collaboration when a project requires fast decision making or high coordination. The company believes remote work can be effective when employees remain visible, responsive, and consistent about their availability.

## Working expectations

Employees who work remotely should keep their calendar up to date, join planned meetings on time, and make their working location and availability visible to their team. Managers are expected to use the same standards for remote and office-based staff so that expectations do not vary based on location alone.

## Collaboration norms

Teams should plan for a mix of synchronous and asynchronous work. For example, project updates should be written down in the shared workspace whenever possible, while quick alignment questions can be handled in chat. The company still prefers a clear handoff and documented decision trail for significant work, even when the people involved are dispersed.

## Team-specific guidance

Some roles, especially client-facing delivery roles and people in the first months of onboarding, may be expected to attend more in-person meetings than others. This should be agreed with the manager rather than treated as an informal exception. The company also encourages employees to use their own judgment about when a short in-person conversation would be more effective than a long remote thread.
