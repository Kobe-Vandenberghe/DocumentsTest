# Decision Making Process

Northstar Digital aims to make decisions in a way that is transparent and timely. The company prefers a lightweight governance model over a large approvals machine. That said, some decisions have real consequences and deserve a documented record.

## Decision categories

- Routine operational decisions can be handled by the relevant lead.
- Cross-functional decisions should be discussed openly and recorded in the team workspace.
- Major strategic or client-impacting decisions should follow a more structured review.

## Recommended approach

When a decision has a cost, timeline, or risk impact, the team should record the context, the options considered, and the chosen path. The [Decision Record Template](../templates/decision-record-template.md) is the preferred format for these situations.

## Practical note

The company still uses some older decision logs that were created before the template was standard. That means not every decision is recorded the same way, but the expectation is that the current team will improve it gradually.
