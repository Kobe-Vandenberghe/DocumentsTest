# Quarterly Planning

Quarterly planning at Northstar Digital is used to coordinate delivery priorities, staffing needs, and internal improvement work. The process is designed to keep the organization focused without turning planning into a large annual exercise.

## Planning inputs

- Client project deadlines and anticipated delivery risks
- Current staffing availability and planned leave
- Internal improvement themes such as documentation, training, or process cleanup
- Security or compliance concerns that need to be addressed soon

## Planning output

Each quarter should end with a short summary of the priority themes, the risks to monitor, and the concrete actions that will be tracked. The planning summary is shared with leadership and core team leads so that the same context is visible across departments.

## Operational rhythm

The planning process usually starts two weeks before the quarter begins. Teams are asked to bring their main risks and dependencies into the planning session rather than waiting for issues to surface later. A short follow-up review is held after the first month to confirm whether the planned actions still make sense.
