# Meeting Guidelines

Northstar Digital uses meetings to coordinate decisions, align stakeholders, and preserve momentum. The company tries to keep meetings short and purposeful, with a clear outcome even when the topic is routine.

## Meeting practices

- Start with a short objective and the expected outcome.
- Share an agenda in advance when the meeting is more than a quick check-in.
- Keep a visible summary of decisions and follow-up actions.
- Avoid using meetings for communication that would be better handled in writing.

## Common meeting types

Weekly project syncs, monthly leadership reviews, and quarterly planning workshops are all standard. Teams often use the [Meeting Notes Template](../templates/meeting-notes-template.md) for cross-functional meetings so that records stay consistent.

## What to avoid

Large status meetings without a clear decision point can become repetitive. The company is still working to standardize the difference between a review meeting and a decision meeting, and that ambiguity is visible in some older notes.
