# Incident Response

Northstar Digital uses a shared incident response process for technical issues that could affect confidentiality, availability, or client trust. The objective is to stabilize the situation quickly, preserve evidence if needed, and communicate clearly with the affected stakeholders.

## Response steps

1. Triage the issue and determine whether it is a security incident, a service outage, or a client-impacting defect.
2. Create a short incident summary and confirm the current impact.
3. Notify the relevant project lead, security officer, and support owner.
4. Work to contain the issue and keep the communication trail updated.
5. Close the incident once the recovery steps and follow-up items are documented.

## Escalation expectations

During business hours, the primary contact is the security officer, Arthur Dubois. For urgent production problems, the engineering lead is also expected to be available. Communication should happen through the incident channel and through the project channel, not through side conversations that leave the record incomplete.

## Communication notes

Detailed incident reports should be written in a concise format with impact, diagnosis, mitigation steps, and follow-up actions. The company also keeps a short incident log for trend analysis. Some teams still use the older contact list for weekend coverage, which sometimes causes confusion during after-hours events.
