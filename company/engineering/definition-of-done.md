# Definition of Done

At Northstar Digital, a piece of work is considered done when it is understandable, validated, and safe to hand over. The checklist below is intended to serve as a baseline rather than an exhaustive contract. Some projects add additional quality gates depending on client requirements or regulatory constraints.

## Baseline checklist

- The requirement is understood and the acceptance criteria are recorded.
- The implementation is reviewed by at least one qualified teammate.
- Relevant tests or validation steps have been completed.
- Documentation has been updated where the change affects future use.
- Any known follow-up tasks are recorded in the tracker.

## Expectations for delivery

Work should be easy to understand for the next person who touches it. That includes meaningful commit messages, clear naming, and enough context for future debugging. If the change affects a shared workflow or a client-facing feature, the project team should also confirm whether any support handover or user communication is needed.

## Known caveat

Some legacy projects still use a slightly older acceptance checklist that does not require the same level of documentation as newer work. That inconsistency is being reduced gradually as teams adopt the newer template.
