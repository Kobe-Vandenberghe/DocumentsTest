# Press Release Template

## Headline

- Draft a concise headline that reflects the announcement.

## Summary

- One paragraph describing the event, milestone, or launch.

## Details

- Include the context, the relevant team, and the business reason for the announcement.

## Quotes

- Add one short quote from a company lead if appropriate.

## Contact

- Include the approved company contact and channel for follow-up.
