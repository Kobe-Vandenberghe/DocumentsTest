# Acceptable Use Policy

The company systems and services provided to employees are intended for business purposes and approved project work. Personal use is acceptable only when it does not interfere with productivity, compromise security, or create unnecessary risk for the company or its clients.

## Expectations

Employees should not use company systems to store personal files that contain sensitive information, bypass security checks, or copy client data into unapproved environments. The use of public AI tools is allowed for general drafting and ideation, but client data should not be pasted into those tools unless there is a documented and approved workflow.

## Handling concerns

If an employee suspects that a tool or workflow might break company policy, they should escalate it before using it in a live setting. Security and legal concerns are more easily managed when they are surfaced early rather than discovered after a release or incident.
