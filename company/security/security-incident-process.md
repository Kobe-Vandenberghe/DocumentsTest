# Security Incident Process

Northstar Digital uses a simple but structured process for handling security events that could affect the company or its clients. The main goals are containment, clear communication, and recovery with minimal long-term damage.

## Immediate steps

- Confirm the nature of the incident and whether it affects a client environment.
- Stop further exposure where possible without creating additional risk.
- Notify the security officer and the impacted project lead.
- Preserve the evidence trail and document what happened.

## Communication and documentation

The security officer is responsible for the initial incident summary, but the project lead and engineering lead should add context from their area of expertise. The incident should be documented in the incident log and linked from the project or client record where appropriate.

## Follow-up expectations

The team should document the root cause, the actions taken, and the preventive steps that will be introduced. If the incident involved a client, the relevant client contact should be informed according to the [Escalation Process](../clients/escalation-process.md).
